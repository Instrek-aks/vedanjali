FAQ

The questions clients ask us first.

Straight answers to help you understand how we work.

What documents do I need to apply for a business loan?
Typically, lenders require 12 months of bank statements, 2-3 years of ITR/financials, GST returns, business registration proof, and promoter KYC. We review and organize all your documents before submission to maximize approval chances.
How long does company registration usually take in India?
Pvt Ltd and LLP registrations generally take 5 to 7 working days once all promoter documents and digital signature certificates (DSC) are ready. We handle the end-to-end filing with MCA and SPICe+ forms.
Can you guarantee that my trademark will be approved?
No consultant or attorney can guarantee trademark approval because the final decision rests with the Trademark Registry. However, we conduct rigorous pre-filing search and classification to minimize objections and prepare strong responses if queries arise.
Do you only work with large companies?
Not at all. We work with early-stage startups, MSMEs, small business owners, as well as established enterprises. Our team tailors solutions according to the size and specific stage of your business.

FAQ

Funding questions, answered honestly.

Straight answers, including the ones other consultants avoid.

What is the difference between a secured and an unsecured business loan?
A secured loan is backed by collateral such as property or machinery, which usually means a larger amount and a lower rate. An unsecured loan needs no collateral, so it is faster to arrange but the amount is smaller and the rate higher. Which one suits you depends on your turnover, your financials and how quickly you need the money.
What is CGTMSE and can my business get collateral free funding?
CGTMSE is a government backed credit guarantee scheme that lets eligible micro and small enterprises borrow without pledging collateral, because the guarantee sits in place of security. Eligibility depends on the sector, entity type and loan size, and we assess that before applying.
How long does business loan approval take?
An unsecured business loan can be sanctioned in one to two weeks when documents are ready. Secured facilities and loan against property take longer because of valuation and legal checks. We tell you the realistic timeline at the assessment stage rather than after you have applied.
Can a new business or startup get funding?
Yes, though the route is different. Without a track record, lenders look at the promoter profile, the project report and the viability of the plan, which is exactly why a properly prepared DPR and financial projection matter so much at this stage.
Do you charge a fee even if the loan is not approved?
Our fee structure is agreed in writing before we begin, including what happens if a case is not sanctioned. You will never discover a charge you were not told about upfront.

FAQ

Registration questions, answered plainly.

Straight answers, including the ones other consultants avoid.

Should I register a private limited company or an LLP?
A private limited company is the right structure if you plan to raise investment, because investors expect shares. An LLP has lighter compliance and suits professional firms and family run businesses that do not intend to raise outside capital. Choosing wrongly is expensive to undo later, so we advise before you file.
How long does company registration take?
Usually seven to fifteen working days once documents are in order. Name approval is the stage that most often causes delay, which is why we check name availability properly before applying.
What is Udyam registration and do I need it?
Udyam is the government registration that formally recognises your business as a micro, small or medium enterprise. It is free, quick, and it unlocks MSME benefits including priority lending, subsidies and protection against delayed payments. Almost every small business should have it.
What is DPIIT recognition and the 80-IAC exemption?
DPIIT recognition marks your company as a recognised startup, which opens access to government schemes and tenders. Section 80-IAC is a separate approval that gives eligible startups a three year income tax exemption. They are applied for separately and the criteria are strict, so we assess eligibility first.
What compliance is required after registration?
Annual returns and financial statements, board and statutory records, GST returns if registered, and director or partner filings. Missing them attracts penalties that accumulate quietly, which is why we offer an ongoing compliance calendar rather than only handling the registration.

FAQ

Trademark and IP questions.

Straight answers, including the ones other consultants avoid.

How long does trademark registration take?
You can use the TM symbol as soon as the application is filed, usually within a few days. Full registration and the R symbol take considerably longer because of examination and publication stages, and objections are common. We handle objections and hearings as part of the service.
What is the difference between TM and R?
TM can be used immediately after filing and signals that you claim the mark. R can only be used once the trademark is formally registered, and it carries far stronger legal protection.
What happens if my trademark gets an objection?
Objections are routine, not a failure. The registry raises a concern and we file a written reply, attend a hearing if required, and argue the case. Handling the objection properly is where most of the value of a trademark service actually sits.
Can I trademark my logo and my name separately?
Yes, and often you should. A word mark protects the name in any styling, while a device mark protects the logo as designed. If the name is the asset, the word mark is usually the priority.
What is the difference between a trademark, a patent and a copyright?
A trademark protects a brand name or logo. A patent protects an invention or a technical process. A copyright protects original creative work such as writing, design, music or code. Many businesses need more than one.

FAQ

Insurance questions, answered plainly.

Straight answers, including the ones other consultants avoid.

What insurance does a small business actually need?
At minimum, cover for the premises and stock, liability cover in case a third party is harmed, and health cover for employees. Beyond that it depends on what you own, who you employ and what you are contractually required to hold.
Is group health insurance compulsory for my business?
Employee health cover is expected under several regulations and is often required by larger clients before they award contracts. Even where it is not mandatory, it is one of the cheapest ways to retain staff.
What is key man insurance?
Cover on the life of a person the business genuinely depends on, usually a founder or a key director. If that person is lost, the payout gives the business time to recover rather than collapsing with them. Lenders and investors often ask about it.
Do you sell the policy or advise on it?
We assess what cover the business actually needs, compare options across insurers and help you place and maintain the policy. The objective is appropriate cover, not the largest premium.
What happens at claim time?
This is where most policies disappoint people, usually because of an exclusion nobody explained at the time of sale. We go through exclusions with you upfront and assist with documentation when a claim arises.

Send us your existing policies. We will tell you what is covered, what is not, and where the real gaps are.
